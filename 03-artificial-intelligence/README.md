# Artificial Intelligence

This section explores artificial intelligence concepts and technologies used to build intelligent applications and practical digital solutions.

The objective is to understand how AI systems can process information, assist users, automate tasks, and support decision-making.

## Areas of Exploration

### 01. AI-Powered Applications

Exploring applications that integrate AI capabilities to provide useful features and improve user experiences.

### 02. Intelligent Assistants

Learning how to build assistants that interpret user requests, retrieve relevant information, and help users complete tasks.

### 03. AI Agents and Automation

Exploring systems that can perform sequences of tasks, interact with tools, and automate selected workflows.

### 04. Data and Knowledge Systems

Investigating how AI can work with structured data, documents, and other information sources to provide useful insights.

### 05. Responsible AI

Understanding the limitations of AI systems, including reliability, bias, privacy, security, and the importance of human oversight.

## Tools and Technologies

Depending on the project, tools may include Python, AI APIs, language models, data processing libraries, and relevant development frameworks.

Technologies will be selected according to each project's objectives and requirements.

## Project Workflow

Projects may involve:

1. Identifying a problem that AI could help solve.
2. Defining the system's requirements and limitations.
3. Selecting suitable models, tools, or services.
4. Implementing and testing the solution.
5. Evaluating its usefulness and reliability.
6. Documenting results, limitations, and improvements.

## Projects

Artificial intelligence projects will be added as ideas are explored and practical solutions are developed.
