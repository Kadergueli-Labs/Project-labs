# Data Science

This section focuses on using data analysis, statistics, and visualization to understand real-world problems and support better decision-making.

## Areas of Interest

### 01. Business Analytics

Analyzing business performance, sales, revenue, customer behavior, and operational efficiency.

### 02. Finance

Exploring financial data, expenditure, profitability, financial indicators, and risk analysis.

### 03. Telecommunications

Analyzing customer behavior, churn, service usage, and telecommunications performance.

### 04. Agriculture

Studying agricultural production, market prices, crop yields, and other indicators related to agricultural systems.

## Project Workflow

Data science projects may include:

1. Defining the problem and objectives.
2. Finding suitable data sources.
3. Cleaning and preparing the data.
4. Exploring and analyzing the data.
5. Creating visualizations.
6. Interpreting results and communicating findings.

## Tools and Technologies

Tools may include Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, SQL, and Jupyter Notebook.

The tools used will depend on each project's requirements.
